# Political Restraint of the Market and Levels of Criminal Homicide: A Cross- National Application of Institutional-Anomie Theory

# Political Restraint of the Market and Levels of Criminal Homicide (Messner and Rosenfeld 1997, Social Forces 75(4): 1393-1416)

Excerpts of the abstract as shown on the University of Missouri-St. Louis research profile page (only these fragments were copied verbatim; the rest of the abstract was not captured):

... we hypothesize that levels of homicide will vary inversely with the "decommodification of labor." ...

The results support our hypothesis and lend credibility to the institutional-anomie perspective.

Context (summary, not quoted): drawing on Esping-Andersen and institutional-anomie theory, the authors measure decommodification from levels and patterns of welfare expenditures and test it in a cross-national multivariate analysis of national homicide rates.
