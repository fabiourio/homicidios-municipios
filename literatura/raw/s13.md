# Social disorganization and homicide mortality rate trajectories in Brazil between 1991 and 2010

Excerpts from the abstract as shown on IDEAS/RePEc (full abstract not reproduced; full text paywalled at doi:10.1016/j.socscimed.2017.08.013).

Social disorganization (SD) has been consistently linked to increases in homicide rates over time

We found that SD is associated with an ascending homicide trajectory.

this is driven by a small proportion of municipalities in the country

Design (summary, not quoted): ecological time series of 4,491 Brazilian municipalities 1991-2010; group-based trajectory models, then logistic regression of trajectory group on a 1991 composite social disorganization index built from socioeconomic and demographic census indicators; SIM age-standardized homicide rates.
