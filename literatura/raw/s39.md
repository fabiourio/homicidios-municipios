# The Crime Kuznets Curve

Excerpts from the abstract (working-paper version, Universidad del Rosario Documentos de Trabajo No. 11083, 2014), as shown on EconPapers. "rst" is the source's dropped "fi" ligature.

As income levels have risen, crime has followed an inverted U-shaped pattern, rst increasing and then dropping.

The Crime Kuznets Curve is not explained by income inequality.

The Crime Kuznets Curve exists for property crime and for some categories of violent crime.
